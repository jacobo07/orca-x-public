<objective>
Bind a LAN Wake Broker target to the identity of the host it wakes, and put that binding on the phone's
`host.wake.targets` answer as an additive optional field.

Purpose: SC1 needs evidence that a card's host is the machine a broker target wakes. The phone already
authenticates every paired host by its E2EE public key, so the binding is that key's fingerprint, entered by the
Owner on the broker (D-01, D-02). This closes 19 D-13 reason 1 at the broker side; the phone side is plan 21-02
(routing) and 21-05 (session wiring).
Output: store, schema, projection, two local-only admin methods, three CLI verbs, tests.
</objective>
