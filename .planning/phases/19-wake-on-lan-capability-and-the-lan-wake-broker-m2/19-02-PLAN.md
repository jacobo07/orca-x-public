<objective>
Build the LAN Wake Broker core: the authority order at the effect, idempotency and adoption, evidence stages, the
magic packet sender, crash-durable targets and grants, and the three phone-facing RPC methods with their refusal
codes passed through.

Purpose: success criterion 2. D-04 (a broker is explicit, otherwise `wake_broker_disabled`), D-05 (targets and MACs
live only on the broker; phones name a broker-issued id), D-06 (standard frame by `dgram`), D-07 (REMOTE_WAKE grant per
device per target, separate from pairing and DeviceScope), D-08 (judged at the effect: device, grant, target),
D-09 (dedupe 60 s, adopt 10 s), D-10 (crash-durable state, revoke drops grants), D-11 (stages; a packet sent is not a
host woken), D-12 (additive methods behind `host.wake-broker.v1`).
Output: shared vocabulary, broker, holder, sender, store with its durability contract, phone methods, tests. This plan
does NOT enable the broker anywhere (19-03 does, behind `--wake-broker`) and does NOT add admin methods or CLI (19-04).
</objective>
