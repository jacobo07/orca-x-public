## Gate rules (what the mjs enforces)

- **G1 coverage:**
  - Every HOST_CARD_LABELS key is classified in `labels`, and every `labels` entry has a key in the table (a stale entry fails).
  - The family must be one of the five. The suffix must belong to the model vocabulary for that family: power -> HOST_POWER_STATES, orca -> HOST_ORCA_STATES, capability -> HOST_CAPABILITY_IDS, capability-value -> HOST_CAPABILITY_VALUES, capabilities -> only `unknown`.
  - `why` is at least 12 characters, and the table holds at least MIN_LABELS keys.
  - The card imports `presentHostCardAvailability` from the presenter.
- **G2 producer:**
  - `producer` is `path#export`. The module exists and exports the symbol (same export regex as the workspace gate), and the presenter imports it.
  - An optional `hostProducer` must exist and export its symbol.
- **G3 evidence:**
  - Every evidence entry is in HOST_EVIDENCE_KINDS.
  - Claim families (power, orca, capability-value) with suffix `unknown` must have `evidence: []`, and their text must match /unknown/i.
  - Every other claim-family entry needs non-empty evidence. Name families (capability, capabilities) carry `evidence: []`.
- **G4 power-loss:**
  - A label whose text matches POWER_LOSS must be keyed `power.sleeping`, `power.hibernating` or `power.powered-off`, with a non-null producer and non-empty evidence.
  - Any string literal or JSX text in the card or presenter outside the HOST_CARD_LABELS block that matches POWER_LOSS fails.
  - Full-line `//` comments and `/* */` blocks are stripped before scanning.
- **G5 controls:**
  - Every `<Pressable`, `<TouchableOpacity`, `<TouchableHighlight` or `<Button` opening tag in the card carries a literal `testID`, classified in `actions` (stale entries fail).
  - `kind: navigation` means the tag's attribute text must not match EFFECT_VERB.
  - `kind: effect` requires an `rpc` that is in the scanned method names.
  - A card string literal or JSX text matching EFFECT_VERB fails unless an effect action is classified.
  - Floors: MIN_PRESSABLES tags and MIN_RPC_METHODS method names.
